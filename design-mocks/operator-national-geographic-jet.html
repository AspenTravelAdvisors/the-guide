Questions people ask

What travelers search for before they book

How much does a National Geographic private jet trip cost?

National Geographic publishes a per-person price for each departure, and it changes with the route and the season. The Guide does not hold rates. An Aspen advisor quotes the current price for your departure and tells you what booking through Aspen adds.

How many guests are on the plane?

The program's published configuration is 75 guests on a Boeing 757-200 with 2×2 leather seating. That is more than Four Seasons (48) or Abercrombie & Kent (50), and fewer than Safrans du Monde (90).

Who travels with you?

National Geographic experts, photographers and expedition staff fly with the group. The lecture program is the reason most people choose this jet over the others.

Where can I see the detailed schedule?

Each itinerary page has the day-by-day plan. Around the World, for example, spends three days at Angkor and two each at Easter Island, Agra, the Serengeti and Petra.

Is there a Silk Road trip?

Yes. The Silk Road by Private Jet is 20 days from Istanbul through Yerevan, Bukhara, Almaty and Dunhuang to Baku. It departs on 27 May 2027.

How far ahead should I book?

A year or more is usual. Each route flies once or twice a year with 75 seats, so there are few seats to go around.
